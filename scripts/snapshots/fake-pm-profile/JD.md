# Senior Product Manager — AI Platform

## Company
Generic AI Platform Inc. (illustrative JD for snapshot generation only)

## Role
We are hiring a Senior Product Manager to lead our AI platform surface. You will own the roadmap, drive cross-functional execution, and shape how AI capabilities land in production for our customers.

## What you will do
- Own a multi-quarter roadmap for our AI platform: model gateway, agent runtime, evaluation harness, and developer-facing SDK.
- Partner with engineering, design, applied research, and trust & safety to ship AI features end-to-end.
- Run experiments and use data to drive prioritization, sizing, and rollout decisions.
- Talk to customers regularly. Translate field signal into a clear product narrative.
- Drive external readiness: docs, demos, and customer-facing release notes.
- Mentor junior PMs and contribute to a healthy, rigorous product culture.

## What we look for
- 7+ years of product management, ideally on platform, AI, AdTech, or developer-tooling surfaces.
- Strong technical depth — you can read model architecture diagrams, debate eval design, and review API specs.
- Track record of shipping AI / ML products to production, not just prototypes.
- Solid understanding of experimentation, guardrails, and post-launch evaluation.
- Excellent written and verbal communication; you can write a 1-pager and a 50-page spec.
- Comfortable with ambiguity; you can scope a vague request into a 2-week deliverable.
- Bonus: experience with LLM agents, MCP, RAG, or evaluation frameworks.

## Nice to have
- Background in AdTech, programmatic, or identity.
- Open-source contributions (especially on agent or evaluation tooling).
- Experience with cross-functional pods of 6–10 people.
