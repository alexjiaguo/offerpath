★★★★★

Action: I spent 3 days building a quick prototype that ran both flows side-by-side on a fake-door test, presented the data, and proposed a compromise…

My biggest design miss at Airbnb

★★★★★
Failure Self-awareness Learning

★★★★★
Leadership Coaching Empathy

Outcome: 14/14 teams shipped on time, 0.3% regression
